======================================================================
2. COLOR SYSTEM — Minimal, restrained
======================================================================

Black   #0d0d0d  — Header bg, footer bg, primary buttons
Off-black #1a1a1a — Body text, section titles
Red     #E02020  — Minimal accent only: "লাইভ" / "ব্রেকিং" badges,
                   thin accent lines, active nav indicator, submit buttons
White   #ffffff  — Page background (dominant surface)
Warm    #f5f5f5  — Alternate section bg, card bg in some contexts
Gray    #999999  — Dates, bylines, metadata
Border  #e0e0e0  — Thin section dividers, card borders

Design principle (NYT-inspired): 95% of the page is black text on white.
Red is used sparingly like a highlighter — only where attention is needed.
No blue, no green, no purple.

NOTE from original plan: Black navbar, footer, category backgrounds are kept
as brand color, but page content area follows NYT's white-dominant approach.


======================================================================
3. TYPOGRAPHY — The backbone of the design (NYT-style)
======================================================================

Masthead / Headlines : Playfair Display (Google Font) — newspaper gravitas
Bangla Body          : Hind Siliguri (Google Font) — best Bangla web font

CRITICAL BANGLA RULES:
  - Body line-height: 1.9 to 2.0 (tight spacing kills readability)
  - Letter-spacing: 0.01em for headlines

FONT SIZE SCALE (NYT-style hierarchy):
  - Masthead (site title): 28–32px
  - Lead story headline: 36–42px
  - Section headlines: 22–26px
  - Secondary story headlines: 18–20px
  - Article body: 17px
  - Metadata (byline, date): 13px
  - Section labels: 11-12px uppercase tracking

NYT INSPIRATION:
  - Headlines are the primary visual element (not images)
  - Sections are separated with thin rules and uppercase section labels
  - Generous whitespace around all text blocks


======================================================================
4. LAYOUT — NYT-inspired flexible grid
======================================================================

NYT uses a fluid multi-column grid (12-column) rather than a rigid 3-column.
Stories can span different widths to create visual hierarchy.

DESKTOP: 12-column CSS grid — flexible story placements
TABLET:  reduced to 6-column grid
MOBILE:  single column

----------------------------------------------------------------------
4.1 HEADER — NYT-style minimal bar
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Background: black #0d0d0d (full width, sticky)
- Height: ~56px (compact, NYT-style)
- Left: "PEN" logo in white, bold serif
- Center: nav links — সংবাদ, প্রশিক্ষণ, আর্কাইভ, নোটিশ, মতামত, ক্যারিয়ার
- Right: search icon, dark mode toggle, login button
- A thin 1px red bottom-border acts as accent line

----------------------------------------------------------------------
4.2 BREAKING NEWS INDICATOR — NYT "LIVE" badge approach
----------------------------------------------------------------------
INSTEAD of a scrolling red bar (original plan), use NYT-style:
- A small red "লাইভ" or "ব্রেকিং" badge next to the relevant story headline
- If a major breaking story is active, a thin yellow/gray bar below the header
  with a single link: "ব্রেকিং: [headline] →" (similar to NYT's breaking bar)
- HIDDEN when no breaking news
- This is much less intrusive while maintaining urgency

----------------------------------------------------------------------
4.3 HERO / TOP STORIES — NYT-style lead story cluster
----------------------------------------------------------------------
NYT approach: ONE dominant lead story + secondary stories in a flexible grid.

- Lead story: large headline, image on top or side, 6-8 columns wide
- Beside/below: 2-4 secondary stories in 4-column cards
- ALL manually curated by editors (no algorithm)
- Each card shows: headline, small image (optional), category tag, timestamp

Layout variations (choose based on content volume):
  A) 1 lead (6 cols) + 2 secondaries (3 cols each) in a row
  B) 1 lead full-width with large image + 3 secondaries in a row below

----------------------------------------------------------------------
4.4 SECTION DIVISION — NYT-style category blocks
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Each section is separated by:
  - A thin 1px border-top line
  - A small uppercase section label (e.g., "শিক্ষা নীতিমালা") in bold, 11px
  - Optional: small red left border on the label

Inside each section:
  - 1 featured story (larger) + 2-3 smaller stories
  - OR if limited content: 3-4 equal cards in a row
  - Minimal card styling — mostly just text with optional small image

----------------------------------------------------------------------
4.5 SIDEBAR — Right rail (NYT-style)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Width: ~300px (fixed) or 30% of content area
- Contains: "পঠিত হচ্ছে" (most-read), upcoming events, district selector
- Separated from main content by a thin left border
- Does NOT scroll independently (simpler responsive behavior)

----------------------------------------------------------------------
4.6 OPINION SECTION — NYT-style distinctive block
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- A separate section with a slightly different visual treatment
- Background tint #f9f9f9 or a thin top border with "মতামত" label
- Cards show: author name + photo (small circle), headline, short excerpt
- Links to full opinion article page
- This visual distinction signals "this is commentary, not news"

----------------------------------------------------------------------
4.7 FOOTER — Dense, information-rich (NYT-style)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Background: black, text: white
- Multi-column layout (4 cols): About PEN, Quick Links, Categories, Contact
- Thin white borders between columns
- Below: copyright line + social media links (Facebook, WhatsApp only)
- "সম্পাদক ও প্রকাশক" and "প্রিন্ট সংস্করণ" links at bottom


======================================================================
5. ARTICLE / CONTENT STRUCTURE
======================================================================

Each article includes:
  - Headline (large Playfair Display)
  - Featured image (16:9 ratio) with caption & photo credit in italic gray
  - Author name & designation
  - Publication date & time
  - Estimated reading time
  - Category tag (small uppercase label)
  - District tag where relevant
  - Rich text body: bold, italic, pull quotes (large serif, stylized),
    embedded images, simple tables
  - "সংশ্লিষ্ট সংবাদ" section at bottom — 3 related articles from same category

ARTICLE PAGE LAYOUT (NYT-inspired):
  - Centered single-column layout for the article body (max ~700px)
  - Headline full width above
  - Image may bleed full width or be contained
  - Byline and date in small gray text below headline
  - Share buttons in a sticky left rail on desktop (Facebook, WhatsApp)

7. FEATURES
======================================================================

----------------------------------------------------------------------
7.1 SEARCH
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Full-text search (Bangla + English)
- Filters: date range, category, district
- Search: icon in header opens a full-page overlay (NYT-style) or dropdown
- Results page: article cards with keyword highlighted

----------------------------------------------------------------------
7.2 USER REGISTRATION & LOGIN
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Register: name, mobile number, district, upazila, school name, designation
- Email: optional
- Login: mobile OTP (primary) + Google login (secondary)
- Dashboard: saved articles, training progress, certificates, notification prefs

----------------------------------------------------------------------
7.3 PEN TRAINING HUB
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Professional development courses
- Each course: title, description, duration, video lessons (YouTube embed)
- Enroll → watch → mark complete → progress bar
- MCQ quiz at end; pass ≥70% → downloadable PDF certificate
  - Certificate: teacher name, course title, completion date, PEN seal
  - Share to WhatsApp
- Physical workshops listed with district-filtered registration forms

----------------------------------------------------------------------
7.4 PEN ARCHIVE
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Searchable document library
- Government circulars, gazette notifications, policy documents, orders
- Organized by YEAR + subcategory: নিয়োগ, বেতন ও ভাতা, ছুটি, বদলি, পরীক্ষা, পাঠ্যক্রম
- Browser PDF viewer or download
- Also: training materials, lesson plans, textbooks by grade

----------------------------------------------------------------------
7.5 EVENTS CALENDAR
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Monthly calendar view
- Types: webinars, workshops, exam dates, scholarship deadlines, holidays, PEN events
- Filterable by district
- Event detail page with "আমার ক্যালেন্ডারে যোগ করুন" → Google Calendar / iCal

----------------------------------------------------------------------
7.6 OPINION & TEACHER VOICE
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Teachers submit own articles → editorial review → publish/reject
- Approved: full name, school, district displayed
- Visually distinct from news (separate section, different card style)

----------------------------------------------------------------------
7.7 COMMENTS
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Moderated, login required, no anonymous
- Reported comments hidden pending review
- Upvote button on comments
- NYT-style: comments section at very bottom, clearly separated

----------------------------------------------------------------------
7.8 NEWSLETTER
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Weekly digest (Sunday), manually curated
- Top 5 articles + 1 event + 1 featured document
- Subscribe via mobile or email
- WhatsApp broadcast as alternative

----------------------------------------------------------------------
7.9 PUSH NOTIFICATIONS
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Browser push for breaking news (opt-in)
- In-app notification bell: new circular, course update, comment reply

----------------------------------------------------------------------
7.10 SOCIAL SHARING
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Facebook + WhatsApp only (Bangladesh-dominant platforms)
- Large thumb-friendly buttons
- Sticky left rail on article pages (NYT-style)

----------------------------------------------------------------------
7.11 DISTRICT FILTER
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Persistent in sidebar
- Filters homepage by district relevance
- Filters events to user's division

----------------------------------------------------------------------
7.12 PRINT VIEW
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Clean print version: no nav, sidebar, ads
- Just: headline, image, body, source info

----------------------------------------------------------------------
7.13 DARK MODE
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Header toggle
- Dark gray bg, white text, red accents unchanged
- Saved to localStorage

----------------------------------------------------------------------
7.14 PWA
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Add to home screen

======================================================================
11. EDITORIAL STANDARDS
======================================================================

- NO auto-playing video
- NO pop-up ads
- MAX 2 ad slots (sidebar + below comments)
- NO clickbait / no unverified rumors
- Official source only for circulars/govt orders
- Named author always (exceptional circumstances only)
- Factual, neutral, precise tone
